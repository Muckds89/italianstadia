Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What this site is

Stadiums of Europe is a free, informational website displaying data about football stadiums across Europe. It is operated as a personal project and has no commercial affiliation with any football club, league, or governing body.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data about visitors. There are no user accounts, no login forms, and no contact forms.

Cookies

This site does not set any first-party cookies. If Google AdSense is active, Google may set advertising cookies to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising via Google's Ads Settings.

Google AdSense

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. For more information, see Google's advertising policies.

Third-party content

Stadium images, team logos, and factual data are sourced from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), Transfermarkt, and other publicly available sources. Coordinates are derived from OpenStreetMap data.

Data export API

The /api/export/stadiums/ endpoint provides factual stadium data (names, capacities, coordinates, ownership) for download. This data consists of publicly available facts and does not contain any personal information.
