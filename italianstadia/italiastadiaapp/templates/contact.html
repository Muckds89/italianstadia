Reporting a data error

Found a wrong capacity, an outdated stadium name, a missing club or an incorrect location? Email us the page URL and what should be corrected, and we'll fix it. Our data is compiled from Wikipedia, Transfermarkt and OpenStreetMap, so accurate corrections with a source are especially welcome.

Suggest a league or stadium

We're continually expanding coverage across Europe. If your league or club isn't here yet, let us know and we'll prioritise adding it.
