About Stadiums of Europe

Stadiums of Europe is a free, interactive map and database of football stadiums across the continent, covering capacities, locations, home clubs, pitch surfaces, ownership and the tournaments each ground hosts.

What you'll find here

  • An interactive map of football stadiums across 30+ European leagues.
  • Detailed pages for every stadium and club we track, including capacity, year built, pitch surface and location.
  • Data insights, from the biggest grounds and retractable roofs to how many clubs each city has.
  • Guides to major tournaments such as UEFA Euro 2032 and their host venues.
  • Stadiums under development across Europe.

Where the data comes from

Factual data, images and coordinates are compiled from publicly available sources, primarily Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), Transfermarkt and OpenStreetMap. We work to keep figures accurate and current, but stadium data changes (renovations, renamings, capacity updates) — if you spot an error, please let us know.

Who runs it

Stadiums of Europe is an independent personal project, not affiliated with any club, league or governing body. It is run by a single football-data enthusiast and funded by unobtrusive advertising to cover hosting costs.
